1. MODULE PURPOSE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The Credit (Udhaar) module manages customer debt tracking, partial bill down
payments, pending credit invoices, subsequent debt installment collections,
FIFO balance allocations across historical sales, transaction-level ledger
auditing, and automated balance recalculation to ensure 100% financial integrity.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
2. FEATURES FOUND
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Cumulative customer debt balance tracking across multiple branch showrooms.
- Filter open debtor sales by store (`Shop 1`, `Shop 2`, or `All`).
- Customer grouping aggregating total pending amounts, open invoice counts,
  and overdue bill counts.
- Search debtors by customer name or phone number.
- Detailed Customer Credit Journal / Ledger:
  - Chronological interleaving of Sales, Down Payments, Subsequent Settlements,
    and Sales Returns.
  - Running balance mathematical progression column.
- Targeted Debt Settlement: Pay against a specific invoice (`saleId`).
- FIFO Unallocated Settlement: General payment automatically applied to oldest
  open invoices first.
- Automated Status Transition: When `remainingBalance` reaches 0, invoice status
  automatically flips from 'Partial' / 'Pending' to 'Full'.
- Validation guards blocking negative or zero payment amounts.
- Master Journal Reconciliation endpoint (`POST /api/recalculate-balances`).

----------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ACTUAL WORKFLOW
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Debt Accrual:
   Customer purchases furniture on Partial or Pending payment. Unpaid balance
   is added to customer running debt in `customers` table and recorded as
   `remainingBalance` on the `sales` record.
2. Debt Review:
   Cashier/Accountant opens "Credit" tab (`currentPage === 'udhaar'`).
   Sees aggregated list of customers with pending balances, sorted by highest
   debtor. Clicks on a customer to open their ledger timeline.
3. Debt Collection / Installment:
   Operator clicks "Record Payment" -> Selects Payment Method (Cash, Bank Transfer,
   Cheque), enters amount, notes, and optional target Invoice ID.
4. Settlement Execution:
   - Writes payment audit record into `payments` table.
   - Decrements customer `balance` in `customers` table.
   - If `saleId` specified: decrements that sale's `remainingBalance`, increments
     its `amountPaid`, and updates `paymentType` to 'Full' if remaining <= 0.
   - If no `saleId`: allocates payment across open sales using FIFO order.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
5. API FLOW
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- GET /api/sales -> Fetches all sales to extract open credit balances.
- GET /api/customers -> Fetches customer profiles and cached debt balances.
- GET /api/payments -> Fetches all payment settlements.
- POST /api/payments -> Accepts `{ customerId, saleId, amount, method, notes }`.
  1. Validates `amount > 0`.
  2. Generates unique payment ID.
  3. Inserts into `payments` table with `createdAt` and `date`.
  4. Fetches customer; decrements `customer.balance` by `amount`.
  5. Updates open sales (either targeted `saleId` or FIFO allocation).
  6. Returns HTTP 201 with payment record.
- POST /api/recalculate-balances -> Recalculates all customer balances from raw
  sales and payments journals.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
6. DATABASE TABLES USED
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- `payments`: Stores subsequent settlement receipts.
- `customers`: Stores customer running debt `balance`, `totalPurchased`, `totalPaid`.
- `sales`: Stores invoice header, `amountPaid`, `remainingBalance`, and `paymentType`.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
7. DATABASE FIELDS INVOLVED
----------------------------------------------------------------------
`payments` table:
- `id`: UUID string.
- `data.customerId`: UUID string.
- `data.saleId`: UUID string (optional).
- `data.amount`: Number.
- `data.method`: 'Cash' | 'Bank Transfer' | 'Cheque' | 'Card'.
- `data.notes`: String.
- `data.date` & `data.createdAt`: Unix timestamp (ms).

`customers` table:
- `data.balance`: Number (net outstanding debt).
- `data.totalPurchased`: Number (gross sales minus returns).
- `data.totalPaid`: Number (sum of upfront payments and subsequent settlements).

`sales` table:
- `data.amountPaid`: Number.
- `data.remainingBalance`: Number.
- `data.paymentType`: 'Full' | 'Partial' | 'Pending'.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
8. DATABASE RELATIONSHIPS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- `payments.customerId` -> `customers.id` (N:1)
- `payments.saleId` -> `sales.id` (N:1, optional)
- Cross-table synchronization: Customer `balance` must equal the sum of
  `remainingBalance` of all open non-quotation sales for that customer.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
9. CREATE / UPDATE / DELETE OPERATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- CREATE (Payment):
  - Inserts new row in `payments`.
  - Mutates `customers` and `sales` rows atomically within the request handler.
- UPDATE:
  - Sales records updated with new `remainingBalance` and `paymentType`.
- DELETE:
  - Payments deletion is restricted to prevent orphan credit adjustments.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
10. BUSINESS LOGIC
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Down-Payment Accounting: Initial checkout payment does NOT create a separate
  row in `payments` table; it is recorded directly in `sale.initialPaid` to avoid
  double-counting.
- Ledger Reversal on Return: Sales returns subtract the refunded amount from customer
  debt and restore inventory.
- Mathematical Invariant:
  $$\text{Customer Balance} = \sum_{\text{open sales}} \text{sale.remainingBalance}$$
- Zero Balance Threshold: When balance drops to $\le 0$, the customer is removed
  from the active debtors list.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
11. CALCULATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Balance Recalculation Formula (PRD FR-CUST-04):
  $$\text{Total Paid} = \text{Settlement Payments} + \text{Upfront Sale Payments} - \text{Return Refunds}$$
  $$\text{Net Purchased} = \text{Total Sales} - \text{Total Returns}$$
  $$\text{Balance} = \text{Net Purchased} - \text{Total Paid}$$
