1. MODULE PURPOSE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The Cashbook module serves as the primary consolidated cash-flow journal,
aggregating all physical and bank transactions into a single unified stream.
It computes real-time daily cash inflows, operational and procurement outflows,
net register cash balances, and provides multi-dimension filtering across
branches, date ranges, and transaction types.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
2. FEATURES FOUND
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Unified Chronological Cash Journal interleaving:
  - Inflows (+): Counter Cash Sales, Down Payments, Customer Debt Installments.
  - Outflows (-): Operating Expenses, Upfront Procurement Cash, Subsequent
    Supplier Payments, and Sales Return Customer Refunds.
  - Informational (0): Inter-shop stock transfers.
- Top KPI Summary Cards:
  - Total Inflow (PKR)
  - Total Outflow (PKR)
  - Net Cash In Register (PKR)
- Quick-Add Operational Expense Modal (`POST /api/expenses`).
- Store location filtering (`All`, `Shop 1`, `Shop 2`).
- Date range filtering (`All Time`, `Today`, `This Week`, `This Month`, Custom).
- Transaction type filtering dropdown (`All Types`, `Sale`, `Payment`, `Expense`,
  `Purchase`, `Supplier Payment`, `Return`, `Transfer`).
- Expense category filtering (Utilities, Rent, Salaries, Transport, Maintenance, etc.).
- Search transactions by invoice number, customer name, supplier name, or description.
- Visual Badges: Green (+) for Cash In, Red (-) for Cash Out, Purple for Stock Transfers.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ACTUAL WORKFLOW
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Inflow Ingestion:
   - When a sale is made with cash paid upfront (`amountPaid > 0`), the cashbook
     records the entry with type "Sale".
   - When a debtor customer pays an installment, `payments` records an entry with
     type "Payment".
2. Outflow Ingestion:
   - When staff logs an operational expense, `expenses` records an entry with type "Expense".
   - When new inventory is procured with cash paid upfront, `purchases` records
     the upfront payment under type "Purchase".
   - When a supplier is paid a subsequent installment, `supplierPayments` records
     an entry under type "Supplier Payment".
   - When a customer returns furniture for a refund, `sales` logs type "Return".
3. Balance Aggregation:
   The UI dynamically computes:
   $$\text{Inflows} = \sum (\text{Sale.initialPaid} + \text{Customer Payments})$$
   $$\text{Outflows} = \sum (\text{Expenses} + \text{Purchase.initialPaid} + \text{Supplier Payments} + \text{Return Refunds})$$
   $$\text{Net Balance} = \text{Inflows} - \text{Outflows}$$

----------------------------------------------------------------------
10. BUSINESS LOGIC
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Immediate Payment Deduplication: When a purchase is created with upfront cash,
  the backend records the purchase in `purchases` and creates an auditable record in
  `supplierPayments` carrying `purchaseId: purchaseId` and `isImmediate: true`.
  The Cashbook deduplication engine checks `!he.isImmediate`, reading `initialPaid` on the purchase,
  guaranteeing that immediate procurement payments are never double-counted, while
  legitimate manual ledger payments with descriptive text are never accidentally suppressed.
- Debt Exclusion: Pending sales with 0 down payment register 0 in the Cashbook.
  Unpaid receivables do not represent liquid cash in hand.
- Non-Cash Transfers: Stock transfers represent internal physical shifts and are
  logged with amount = 0, ensuring net cash register figures are never distorted.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
11. CALCULATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Inflow Calculation:
  $$\text{Inflow} = \text{Cash Sales} + \text{Down Payments} + \text{Customer Debt Settlements}$$
- Outflow Calculation:
  $$\text{Outflow} = \text{Expenses} + \text{Procurement Upfront} + \text{Supplier Payments} + \text{Refunds}$$
- Net Cash Register:
  $$\text{Net Balance} = \text{Inflow} - \text{Outflow}$$
