1. OVERVIEW & METHODOLOGY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
In a production enterprise retail system, modules do not operate in isolation.
A counter sale alters inventory stock, customer credit ledgers, cashbook streams,
and executive profitability reports simultaneously.

This audit executes controlled end-to-end multi-module workflows and records
the exact state of every affected SQLite table before and after the action.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WORKFLOW 1: FULL CASH COUNTER SALE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Workflow Path:
  POS Cart -> `sales` -> `products` (Stock -Qty) -> `counters` -> `cashbook` (+Cash) -> `reports` (+Rev)

Logical Before Snapshot:
  - Product "Royal Oak King Bed" Stock = 10 units
  - Sales Count = 0
  - Cashbook Inflows = 0 PKR
  - Invoice Counter = 0

Action:
  Cashier sells 2 units of "Royal Oak King Bed" @ 65,000 PKR = 130,000 PKR total.
  Payment Type = "Full", Amount Paid = 130,000 PKR. Walk-in Customer.

Logical After Snapshot:
  - Product "Royal Oak King Bed" Stock = 8 units (Exact -2 units)
  - Sales Table = 1 new row (Invoice #1, total: 130,000, remaining: 0)
  - Invoice Counter = 1
  - Cashbook Inflows = +130,000 PKR (Categorized under "Sale")
  - Reports Revenue = +130,000 PKR, Confirmed Orders = 1

Database Reconciliation Verification:
  [PASS] Physical stock verified in SQLite: 10 - 2 = 8.
  [PASS] Cashbook inflow matches amountPaid: 130,000 PKR.
  [PASS] Zero customer debt created.

3. WORKFLOW 2: PARTIAL CREDIT SALE (DOWN PAYMENT + DEBT ACCRUAL)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Workflow Path:
  POS Cart -> `sales` -> `products` (Stock -Qty) -> `customers` (+Debt) -> `cashbook` (+DownPay) -> `reports` (+Rev)

Logical Before Snapshot:
  - Product Stock = 8 units
  - Customer "Mian Tariq Jamil" Balance = 0 PKR
  - Cashbook Inflows = 130,000 PKR

Action:
  Customer buys 1 unit of "Royal Oak King Bed" for 65,000 PKR.
  Payment Type = "Partial", Down Payment Paid = 25,000 PKR, Remaining Debt = 40,000 PKR.

Logical After Snapshot:
  - Product Stock = 7 units (Exact -1 unit)
  - Sales Table = 1 new row (Invoice #2, total: 65,000, amountPaid: 25,000, remaining: 40,000)
  - Customer Record in SQLite:
    - `balance` = 40,000 PKR (Exact match with remainingBalance)
    - `totalPurchased` = 65,000 PKR
    - `totalPaid` = 25,000 PKR
  - Cashbook Inflows = 130,000 + 25,000 = 155,000 PKR (ONLY the 25,000 PKR down payment is added)
  - Reports Revenue = 130,000 + 65,000 = 195,000 PKR (Full invoice value recognized)

Database Reconciliation Verification:
  [PASS] Debt accrual exactly equals `invoice.total - invoice.amountPaid`.
  [PASS] Cashbook registers liquid cash received, NOT unearned receivables.
  [PASS] Reports recognize earned sales revenue.

  [PASS] Customer debt ledger accurately reverses to 0.
  [PASS] Executive reports accurately adjust net revenues.
